JobAI Privacy Policy 私隱政策

Last updated / 最後更新:2026-07-07

1. Introduction 簡介

JobAI ("we") provides AI-powered job search services. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it. JobAI(「我們」)提供 AI 求職服務,本政策說明我們收集嘅資料同用途。

2. Data We Collect 我們收集嘅資料

  • Account 帳戶資料: name, email, phone 姓名、電郵、電話
  • Profile & CV 履歷資料: work history, education, skills 工作經驗、學歷、技能
  • Media 媒體: photos and interview videos you upload 你上傳嘅相片同面試視頻
  • Usage 使用數據: app interactions for improving the service 用於改善服務嘅使用記錄
  • Payment 付款: processed by third-party providers (e.g. Stripe); we do not store card numbers 由第三方(如 Stripe)處理,我們唔會儲存卡號

3. How We Use Data 資料用途

  • Matching you with jobs and employers 職位同僱主配對
  • Generating AI resumes, cover letters and interview feedback 生成 AI 履歷、求職信同面試反饋
  • Facilitating video interviews you initiate 進行你發起嘅視頻面試
  • Service improvement and support 改善服務同提供支援

4. Sharing 資料分享

Your profile and interview videos are shared with employers only when you apply or consent. We never sell your personal data. 只有喺你申請職位或同意時,你嘅資料先會分享俾僱主。我們絕不出售你嘅個人資料。

5. Camera & Microphone 相機同麥克風

Camera and microphone are used only for video interviews and profile media, always initiated by you. 相機同麥克風只用於你主動發起嘅視頻面試同個人資料媒體。

6. Data Retention & Deletion 資料保留同刪除

Data is retained while your account is active. You can delete your account in-app (Settings → Delete Account); all personal data is permanently removed within 30 days. 帳戶活躍期間資料會保留。你可以喺 App 內(設定 → 刪除帳戶)刪除帳戶,所有個人資料會喺 30 日內永久移除。

7. Security 安全

All data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored with industry-standard safeguards. 所有資料經 HTTPS 傳輸,並以行業標準措施儲存。

8. Children 兒童

JobAI is not directed at children under 16. JobAI 唔適用於 16 歲以下人士。
